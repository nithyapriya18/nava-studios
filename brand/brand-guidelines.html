Indigo & Marigold

Indigo is the oldest dye India gave the world: deep, steady and trustworthy. Marigold is the colour of beginnings: warm, local and impossible to ignore. Together they say a senior builder from Bengaluru who is good company.

classy on the page, fun in the margin

01 · Idea

A page, and a margin

Founders hiring a solo builder ask two things: can I trust this person with my idea, and will working with them feel human? The page answers the first with structure, real numbers, fixed prices and strong colour blocks. The margin answers the second with pen notes in vermilion red and marigold circles, like someone marked up your plan.

The brand tells the story of a project: napkin, then scope, then shipped. The hand is loudest at the start and quietest at the end. Finished things look finished.

04 · Schemes

Light and dark

Every screen ships in both schemes, because not everyone likes a dark background, and not everyone likes a bright one. Pages follow the device’s setting until someone chooses; a round sun and moon button in the header switches scheme, and the choice is remembered. Try it at the top right of this page.

The type, marks, layout and voice stay the same. Only the colour of each role changes. Light means light: bands become Indigo wash (#EEEFFA) and the footer is white, with indigo in the headlines, accents and buttons. In the dark scheme, bands are Night indigo, marigold takes over as the accent and the button, and the notes use a lighter vermilion.

06 · Type

Three voices, three typefaces

Display · Instrument SerifSoftware people use.
UseHeadlines, big numbers, step numerals, FAQ questions, post titles. The italic word takes the accent colour. Never below 22px.
Page · Instrument SansI design and build software for founders and small businesses, for a fixed price agreed before work starts.
UseBody 17/1.6, small 14–15, labels 12 uppercase at 0.1em tracking. Every button, field and nav item.
Margin · Kalam Lightthe price is set here, once
UseMargin notes only: vermilion on Paper, marigold on Indigo, 18–24px, one short line.

07 · The margin

Four pen marks, used sparingly

This is the whole budget for fun. One mark per screen view. If a mark doesn’t point at something true and specific, delete it.

Circle · marigold

From a napkin sketch

For the one word a page is about. Hero headlines only.

Underline · marigold

One fixed price

For a promise. One section heading per page.

Margin note · vermilion

a few lines is enough to start

An aside in your voice, beside what it explains. Under eight words.

Correction · vermilion

hourly billing

For what the studio doesn’t do, or what waits until later. Rare.

MotionCircles and underlines draw on once, 900ms. Nothing loops. With reduced motion turned on they’re simply there. Marks are absolutely positioned, so they never shift layout.

08 · Controls

Buttons, links and fields

Controls belong to the page, never the margin: round, exact, never drawn. Primary is Indigo on Paper and Marigold on Indigo; hover swaps to the other brand colour.

09 · Layout

Rhythm and colour blocks

Page rhythmIndigo hero → Paper sections → a Turmeric band for proof → Paper → Indigo contact band → Deep Indigo footer. Every page opens on a colour block, and no stretch of plain paper runs longer than about two screens.
Spacing8px base. Sections 72px apart on desktop, 48px on phones. A 200px label rail on desktop; everything stacks at phone width with a 20px gutter.
SurfacesWhite cards with a 1px border and 16px radius for grouped items. Panels sitting on Indigo (forms, the product demo) get a soft deep shadow. 999px radius for buttons.
ImageryOne real portrait, ideally on a marigold or indigo backdrop. No stock photos, no illustrated people, no gradients or glows.
UI library picksAccordion (shadcn) for FAQs · Sheet (shadcn) for the phone menu · Stateful Button (Aceternity) for forms · Timeline (Aceternity), static, on About.

10 · Voice

How it sounds

Plain, direct, first person. Say what it costs and what you get; skip what you are.

Person“I design and build…”not “We craft bespoke digital experiences”
Price“a fixed price agreed before work starts”not “affordable packages”
Proof“$6.4M documented cost impact”not “proven track record”
Limits“If you need very large scale from day one, I’ll say so.”not “No project too big or small”
Marginthe price is set here, oncenot jokes, emoji or exclamation marks

11 · Checks

Do and don’t

Do
  • Open every page on a colour block: Indigo wash in light, Night indigo in dark
  • Use one pen mark per view, pointing at something true
  • Keep finished UI crisp; the hand belongs to drafts and notes
  • Ship both schemes, with the sun / moon toggle in the header
  • Check every page in light, in dark, and at 390px
Don’t
  • Draw buttons, fields or the wordmark by hand
  • Put marigold text on Paper (it fails contrast)
  • Add gradients, glows, emoji or stock imagery
  • Animate anything that loops or follows the cursor
