# Verity Studio — Positioning

## What am I? (the one-line answer)

**Verity Studio is a one-person software studio that builds small, fair-priced tools
for small businesses — the problems too small for big software companies to bother with.**

You are not "a SaaS company with 25 products." You are **the small-software studio**.
One person, many small tools, one promise: *each tool does one job, costs less than the
problem it solves, and takes minutes to start using.*

## Positioning statement

> For small business owners drowning in overpriced, overcomplicated software,
> Verity Studio makes single-purpose tools that pay for themselves in the first week —
> priced so you never have to think twice.

## Taglines (pick one, use everywhere)

1. **"Small software. Fair prices. One job each."** ← recommended
2. "Tools too small for big companies to build."
3. "Software that pays for itself in a week."

## The identity people should associate you with

People can't remember 25 products. They CAN remember:
- **A person**: "Nithya — the solo founder who ships a small business tool every few weeks."
- **A promise**: "Everything she makes is simple and fairly priced."
- **A story**: "Big software abandoned small businesses. She builds what they won't."

Your personal brand IS the marketing engine. Solo + prolific + honest pricing is a
story tech media and communities love. Lean into it, never hide it.

## The 5 product families (how the 25 group)

Group by the *job the buyer needs done*, not by industry. A buyer should find their
family in 5 seconds.

### 1. Get Paid (8) — "money in the door"
GST Calculator · GST WhatsApp Biller · One-Page Invoice · Follow-Up Fox ·
Trade Quote · Proposal Forge · Course Ship · Member Gate

### 2. Know Your Numbers (6) — "see where money leaks"
Margin Lens · Etsy Ledger · Rent Ledger · Stock Sentinel · Ship Watch · Uptime Peek

### 3. Win Customers (5) — "turn interest into revenue"
Nudge Book · Quote Card · Testimonial Jar · Review Radar · Report Snap

### 4. Run the Day (4) — "stop drowning in busywork"
Doc Chaser · Scope Guard · Tiny CRM · Crew Board

### 5. Verity Home (2) — "made for my own family"
EasyCook · SnuggleFox

**Verity Home is deliberate, not an inconsistency.** These two humanize the brand:
"I build tools for your business, and I build software for my own family with the
same care." Never present them as part of the business thesis; present them as
proof of craft.

## Messaging house

|---|---|
| Roof (brand promise) | Small software. Fair prices. One job each. |
| Pillar 1 | **Honest pricing** — flat, low, no per-seat traps, free tiers that are actually useful |

## Boilerplate (copy-paste)

**Short (bio):** I run Verity Studio — a one-person software studio building small,
fair-priced tools for small businesses. One job each. No bloat, no enterprise pricing.

**Medium (about page / press):** Verity Studio is a one-person software studio.
Big software companies chase enterprise deals and leave small businesses with tools
that are too expensive, too complex, or both. Verity builds the small stuff they
won't: single-purpose tools for getting paid, knowing your numbers, winning customers,
and running the day — each priced so a small business never has to think twice.

## Rules that protect the positioning

1. **Never launch a tool without a public price.** Vague pricing kills the "fair" pillar.
2. **Every product page leads with the problem in the buyer's words** (from the forum
   research), not features.
3. **If a tool needs a manual to use, it's off-brand.** Cut features until it doesn't.
4. **Status badges stay honest** (Live / Beta / Building). Trust is the moat.
