# Collateral Kit — every template you need, ready to fill

All collateral is text + product screenshots/GIFs. Nothing here needs a designer.
GIFs: record the screen doing the one job (macOS: Cmd+Shift+5 → trim → gif). 15s max.

## 1. Product one-liner formula (used on cards, bios, posts)

`[Tool] — [does the one job] for [who], without [the thing they hate].`
Example: *Follow-Up Fox — chases your overdue invoices with polite automatic
reminders, without you writing another awkward email.*

## 2. Launch post (X/LinkedIn/IndieHackers) — per product

```
Small businesses told me [pain, in their words — quote a real thread].

Existing tools want $[X]/mo for a suite. This problem needs one screen.

So I built [Tool]: [one-liner].
- [thing 1 it does]
- [thing 2]
- [price, flat, visible]

Free tier: [what's free]. Built in [N] days as part of my 25-tools-in-public run.
[link] — I answer every reply.
```

## 3. Founding-customer DM/email (B2B tools: Doc Chaser, Report Snap, Nudge Book, Crew Board)

```
Subject: [their pain in 5 words] — built something for it

Hi [name], I saw [specific: their firm size / a post / their site].

[One sentence naming the pain: "Most bookkeeping firms I talk to lose hours every
month chasing clients for bank statements."]

I'm a solo founder and just built [Tool] — [one-liner]. I'm taking 10 founding
customers: free setup + half price for 3 months, in exchange for blunt feedback.

Worth a 15-min look? [link] — no pressure either way.
— Nithya, Nava Studios
```

## 4. Community answer pattern (Reddit/forums — 3/week)

1. Answer the actual question fully (this is 80% of the post).
2. Then: *"Disclosure: I built a small tool for exactly this ([Tool], [price]) after
   seeing this complaint everywhere — [link]. But honestly [free alternative] also
   works if you only need X."*
The honest alternative-mention is what keeps you welcome (and it converts better).

## 5. Product page FAQ starters (adapt per product)

- What does it cost? → [flat price]. No per-seat, no surprise tiers.
- Is there a free tier? → [exactly what's free].
- Where's my data? / Can I export it? → [answer + one-click export]
- What happens if I cancel? → [data retention answer, refund policy link]
- Who's behind this? → One person — Nithya. You'll get replies from me, not a bot.

## 6. Testimonial ask (day 14 after first payment — automate via Follow-Up Fox pattern)

```
Hi [name] — you've been using [Tool] for two weeks. Two quick questions:
what was the moment it earned its price, and what still annoys you?
If the first answer is good, may I quote you? Takes 60 seconds: [Testimonial Jar link]
```

## 7. Monthly build-in-public update (last Friday of the month)

Numbers first (visitors, signups, MRR — real, even when embarrassing), then:
what shipped, what flopped, what's next. Same structure every month; the series
becomes the story people follow.

## 8. Press/launch kit (one page at /about#press — needed before Product Hunt)

- 2-sentence boilerplate (in positioning.md)
- Founder photo + name + location
- Logo + product screenshots (light/dark)
- The narrative hook: "solo founder shipping 25 small-business tools in public"
- Contact: hello@

## 9. Product Hunt launch (per family, ~5 total)

- Tagline = the family promise ("Get paid without thinking about it")
- Gallery: 1 GIF per tool in the family + 1 pricing screenshot (flat prices are a PH applause line)
- First comment: the research story — "I read 1,000+ complaints from small business
  owners; these tools are what they asked for" + the honest pricing pledge.
- Launch Tue–Thu, be online all day replying.
