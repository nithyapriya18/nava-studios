## What it is

EasyCook plans a week of meals from what the household actually eats: preferences, allergies, and which nights need something quick.

I built it for my own kitchen. It is a personal project, not a client product. I keep it because I use it.

## What it does

- A week of meals matched to those constraints
- Recipes with prep notes, and a way to mark a meal as cooked
- Swaps that respect the allergy list
- Onboarding that can start from a meal you already cook
