## What it is

EasyCook plans a week of meals from what the household actually eats. It takes preferences and allergies into account, and knows which nights need something quick.

I built it for my own kitchen and I still use it.

## What it does

- Plans a week of meals around those constraints
- Gives recipes with prep notes, and lets you mark a meal as cooked
- Suggests swaps that respect the allergy list
- Can start from a meal you already cook
