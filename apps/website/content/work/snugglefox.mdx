## What it is

SnuggleFox writes a short bedtime story from what a parent types in: the child’s name, age, favourite things, and optionally a detail from the day. The parent owns the account and the inputs.

I built it for bedtime at home. It is a personal project, not a children’s brand and not a client product.

## How it works

- A parent describes the child (name, age, favourite things)
- Optionally adds a detail from today
- The story is meant to wind down, not to cliffhang

Stories are generated text. A parent should read them before sharing them with a child.
