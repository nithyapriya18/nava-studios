## What it is

A small web app I built for Indian GST math: tax-exclusive to final amount, tax-inclusive back to taxable value, CGST + SGST vs IGST, and an itemised bill with live totals.

I still use it when a quote needs a number quickly. It is a personal project, not a client product.

## What it does

- Forward calculation (exclusive and inclusive)
- Reverse calculation from an inclusive total or a GST amount
- Intra-state vs inter-state from the state you pick
- Itemised bill with a copyable summary

Figures from this tool are estimates. Confirm important numbers with your accountant.
