## What it is

A small web app for Indian GST. It works out the final amount from a tax-exclusive price, gets back to the taxable value from an inclusive one, splits CGST and SGST or applies IGST, and totals an itemised bill as you type.

I built it for my own quotes, and I still use it when a quote needs a number quickly.

## What it does

- Forward calculation, exclusive or inclusive
- Reverse calculation from an inclusive total or a GST amount
- Intra-state or inter-state, based on the state you pick
- An itemised bill with a summary you can copy

The figures are estimates. Check important numbers with your accountant.
