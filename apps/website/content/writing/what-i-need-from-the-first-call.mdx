title: "What I need from the first call"
date: "2026-08-12"
excerpt: "Bring the idea as it stands. The call is to agree what v1 is, so a quote can be honest."
tags: ["Engagements"]
---

The first call is unpaid. It is not a free build. It exists so we can both see the same first version, and so I can send a quote that matches it.

You can arrive with a long spec. You can arrive with a voice note. You can arrive with “I want something like X, for Y people.” I will take all of those. I will not ask you to throw the spec away, and I will not pretend a spec is already a product.

What I am listening for is simpler than a full requirements document:

- Who it is for
- What they need to complete
- What you already have (copy, brand, data, a half-built repo)
- What “working” means in a few weeks, not in a year

I write that back as scope. Then a number. Paid work starts when you accept that number.

If the idea is still a feeling, the call is still useful. We will leave with a smaller sentence than we entered with, or we will leave knowing it is too early. Either is a decent outcome. The expensive path is starting a build on a sentence neither of us could repeat.
