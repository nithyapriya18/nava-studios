## The problem

"What do we eat tomorrow?" is a small question that costs households a
surprising amount of energy — asked every single day, usually at the worst
time, and constrained by allergies, preferences, and whoever gets home late.

## What I built

EasyCook learns the household once — who eats what, what's off the table,
which nights need something quick — then plans the week:

- A full week of meals matched to the household's real constraints
- Recipes with prep notes and a mark-as-cooked flow
- Swaps that respect the allergy list (not just "here's a different dish")
- Onboarding that starts from the meal you already know you're making

This is one of two **Verity Home** products — software I built for my own
family first, with the same care that goes into the business tools.

## Status

In beta, feeding my own household weekly.
