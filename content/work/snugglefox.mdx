## The problem

Every parent knows the fourth "one more story" of the night. The books have all
been read twice, the invented plots are running thin, and it's 9:40pm.

## What I built

SnuggleFox generates personalised bedtime stories: the child's name, the things
they love, and even something from their day get woven into a gentle,
age-appropriate story. Parents create and control everything — it's their
account, their inputs, their call.

This is one of two **Verity Home** products — software I built for my own family
first, with the same care that goes into the business tools.

## How it works

- A parent describes the child (name, age, favourite things)
- Optionally adds a detail from today
- SnuggleFox writes a calm, kind story shaped for bedtime — no scary turns,
  no cliffhangers, designed to wind a child down rather than up

## Status

In beta with my own household as user zero.
